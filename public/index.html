How it works

  1. Connect Strava

    Sign in once. Your Strava tokens stay encrypted on the server and never reach the browser.

  2. Your runs become metrics

    Each session is turned into training load, aerobic efficiency, cardiac drift and time in heart-rate zones.

  3. Ask the coach

    Describe a session in your own words. The coach finds it and answers with your numbers, not generic advice.

What it measures

Aerobic efficiency

Efficiency Factor (metres per minute per heartbeat) on comparable flat easy runs. It shows whether your base endurance is improving, whatever the day’s pace.

Load and risk

Banister TRIMP and the acute:chronic workload ratio (ACWR). It flags sudden jumps in load, the most common cause of overuse injuries.

Cardiac decoupling

Second-by-second analysis of how your heart rate drifts at a steady pace, plus time spent in each zone, from Strava streams.

A coach grounded in data

The model only sees metrics computed on the server, never data sent from the browser. Every piece of advice cites the numbers behind it.
