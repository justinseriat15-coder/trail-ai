TRAIL.AI

Endurance analytics · Strava · Claude

Your training,
read like a coach.

Trail.AI turns Strava data into endurance physiology metrics, then gives an AI coach only those computed numbers. You write "heavy legs this morning", it finds the session, cross-checks it against your load and answers.

Explore the demo Connect Strava

The demo runs on a fictional athlete (16 weeks of realistic synthetic data). Strava login is limited to the owner while the app is in Strava developer mode.

Aerobic efficiency

Efficiency Factor (m/min per heartbeat) on comparable flat easy runs. It tracks base endurance progress, independent of the day's pace.

Load and risk

Banister TRIMP, then acute:chronic workload ratio (ACWR). It flags load spikes that are too sudden, the #1 cause of overuse injuries.

Cardiac decoupling

Second-by-second analysis: HR drift at constant pace (Pa:HR) and time in zones, computed from Strava streams.

Grounded AI coach

The model only sees metrics computed server-side, never data sent by the browser. Every piece of advice cites its numbers.
